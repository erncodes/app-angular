Quick Action Guide

Void :

To void an item press letter 'v' followed by the item's barcode then press 'Enter'.

Read :

To read an item press letter 'r' followed by the item's barcode then press 'Enter'.

Add to cart :

To add an item to cart, enter item's barcode on the 'main input' then press 'Enter' or click on the item in the popular products panel.

SubTotal Mode :

To void an item press '+' on numeric keyboard to enter subtotal mode.

Toggle Panel :

Press Space bar to toggle panel or use the UI controls.

Finalise sale :

Enter subtotal mode first(press '+') then enter amount followed by '-' to finilise sale. If the amount entered is less than the cart's total, then amount will be subtracted and the POS will remail in 'Final Mode' until the required amount is entered.
